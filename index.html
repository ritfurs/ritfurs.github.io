About RitFurs

Welcome to RitFurs, RIT's premier unofficial furry community!

We organize regular meetups, hangouts, and activities where members can connect, share their creativity, and celebrate being furry in a welcoming environment.

Whether you're an artist, a writer, a fursuiter, or simply curious about the fandom, you'll find a place here. Join us for game nights, meetups, conventions, and more!

Our community is built on respect, creativity, and friendship.
